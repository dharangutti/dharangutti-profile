  • Future Smartphone Enhanced Open Innovation Concepts
    4 September 2026 · Working paper · v2.0 · CC BY 4.0

    This paper proposes three independently implementable concepts for future smartphones, sharing a common philosophy of treating the physical device itself as a programmable computing resource rather than limiting innovation to the display and application layer. The concepts explore new ways of extending smartphone capabilities through programmable physical-device architecture, creating opportunities for enhanced sensing, computation, interaction, and functionality beyond conventional software-defined features.

  • A Star-Inspired Framework for Transforming Nuclear Waste at the Source
    23 August 2026 · Working paper · v1.0 · CC BY 4.0

    Long-lived radioactive waste is a consequence of nuclear fission, with a small fraction of spent-fuel inventory accounting for a disproportionately large share of long-term radiotoxicity and stewardship burden. This paper proposes a conceptual framework inspired by the way stars transform nuclei: selected radionuclides are partitioned, recycled, transmuted, and finally immobilized in durable wasteforms across a three-layer architecture combining actinide recycling, targeted transmutation, and high-durability immobilization.

  • Flux Logic Computing
    18 July 2026 · Working paper · v2.0 · CC BY 4.0

    A conceptual digital logic family in which binary information is represented by deterministic magnetic flux patterns rather than voltage ranges. Defines two canonical flux states, a gate abstraction based on pattern recognition and Boolean evaluation, and a propagation model, with explicit axioms and success criteria for future validation.

  • Seabed-Anchored Adaptive Ocean Energy Network
    15 July 2026 · Working paper · v1.0 · CC BY 4.0

    A conceptual marine-energy architecture placing power-take-off, generator, and grid-forming infrastructure on the seabed while lightweight hydrodynamic collectors operate on controlled high-tension tethers. Covers operating modes, PTO options, control architecture, engineering constraints, and a staged validation pathway from analytical modelling to open-sea demonstrators.
