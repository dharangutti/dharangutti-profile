  • AURORA-LEO Adaptive Multi-Satellite Broadband Through Resource-Aware Link Selection
    19 November 2026 · Working paper · v1 · CC BY 4.0
    DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.24356293

    This paper proposes AURORA-LEO (Adaptive Unified Radio and Orbital Routing Architecture), a resource-aware framework for adaptive multi-satellite broadband connectivity in Low Earth Orbit (LEO) networks. The architecture combines capability-based terminal admission, predictive mobility, candidate/prepared/active satellite link states, packet convergence, and gateway-aware orbital routing. Rather than maintaining a fixed number of simultaneous satellite connections, AURORA-LEO dynamically selects links based on available capacity, interference, energy consumption, coordination overhead, and shared network bottlenecks. Hypothetical numerical illustrations demonstrate why additional satellite links do not necessarily increase delivered throughput. The paper presents a constrained network-utility model, comparative evaluation scenarios, and a staged experimental validation methodology for future LEO broadband systems.

  • Continuous Electromagnetic Matter Interrogation Across Conventional Spectral Boundaries
    19 September 2026 · Working paper · v1.0 · CC BY 4.0
    DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.22841760

    This paper proposes Continuous Electromagnetic Matter Interrogation (CEMI), implemented conceptually as a federated Electromagnetic Tuning Array (ETA). Neighboring measurement modules retain source, optics, and detector technologies appropriate to their spectral intervals while being connected through deliberate spectral overlap, cross-calibration, quantified uncertainty, and a common photon-energy-indexed data model. A first demonstration is proposed across approximately 70–200 eV using aluminium and silicon L-shell absorption-edge regions as calibration landmarks.

  • The Deployable Smartphone
    12 September 2026 · Working paper · v1.0 · CC BY 4.0
    DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.22727729

    This paper proposes an architecture-level framework for mechanically deployable mobile devices. A compact computing core remains available in every state, while task-specific physical capabilities deploy when useful. It explores pivot deployment, slide and fold, functional folding wings, and deployable instrument modules, supported by a mode contract, physical capability API, and scorecard for selecting coherent device configurations.

  • A Star-Inspired Framework for Transforming Nuclear Waste at the Source
    23 August 2026 · Working paper · v1.0 · CC BY 4.0
    DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.22066928

    Long-lived radioactive waste is a consequence of nuclear fission, with a small fraction of spent-fuel inventory accounting for a disproportionately large share of long-term radiotoxicity and stewardship burden. This paper proposes a conceptual framework inspired by the way stars transform nuclei: selected radionuclides are partitioned, recycled, transmuted, and finally immobilized in durable wasteforms across a three-layer architecture combining actinide recycling, targeted transmutation, and high-durability immobilization.

  • Flux Logic Computing
    18 July 2026 · Working paper · v2.0 · CC BY 4.0
    DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.21446058

    A conceptual digital logic family in which binary information is represented by deterministic magnetic flux patterns rather than voltage ranges. Defines two canonical flux states, a gate abstraction based on pattern recognition and Boolean evaluation, and a propagation model, with explicit axioms and success criteria for future validation.
